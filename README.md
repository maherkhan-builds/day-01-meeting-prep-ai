# 🚀 Meeting Prep AI  
**Part of my AI App Building Challenge (Vibe Coding)**

---

## ❓ The Problem

Professionals waste **20+ minutes before every meeting** scrambling through emails, Slack, and documents.

They often show up:
- Unprepared  
- Reactive  
- Anxious  
- Without structured talking points  

Time is lost. Confidence drops. Meetings underperform.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**Meeting Prep AI** generates structured meeting preparation in **under 30 seconds**:

- 3–5 key talking points  
- 3 intelligent questions to ask  
- 2–3 potential objections with responses  
- A confidence-boosting opening line  

The result: clarity, structure, and professional confidence.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Enter meeting topic  
2. Add attendees + your role  
3. Define meeting goal  
4. Click **Generate Prep**  

AI generates a structured, ready-to-use meeting brief instantly.

---

## 🧪 Prototype

Built using **Google Stitch** and enhanced with Gemini-based prompt logic.

This is a **working prototype**, designed to demonstrate rapid AI-powered app development.

---

## 🧾 The Exact Prompt I Used

~~~txt
Create a web app called "Meeting Prep AI". The app should have: 
- A clean, professional interface 
- Input fields for: meeting topic, attendees, your role, and meeting goal 
- A "Generate Prep" button 
- Output sections for: key talking points (3-5), smart questions to ask (3), potential objections with responses (2-3), and a confidence-boosting opening line. 
Use Gemini API to generate responses based on the inputs. 
Make it mobile-friendly and use a modern, minimal design with Google's Material Design principles. 
Include a "Copy to Clipboard" button for each section.
~~~
